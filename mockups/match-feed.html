    Who could be pressured, and what they'd hand over

    Video host: can be ordered to remove a video, or hand over account data, as happened to a Mastodon server in 2023. Mirrors survive. It never holds watch history.

    Other viewers: with P2P streaming on, they can see your IP for each video. That's why it's off by default.

    Relay: holds ciphertext. Gift-wrapping hides the sender and fixed-size sketches on a schedule hide activity, but timing can still link people. Mitigation: Oblivious HTTP now, mixnet transport later.

    Your device: holds the plaintext history. Seizure exposes it. Mitigation: encryption at rest, retention windows, wipe.

    A match: sees what you shared. That is the point of matching, so this one can't be engineered away.

    Who can't steer the feed

    No server ranks. Your ranker reads only peers you chose. A bot farm has to get you to match its accounts before it can move your feed.
